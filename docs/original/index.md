# Original analysis (upstream)

These pages are the DPOA collaboration's analysis **as originally written**, kept unchanged for
provenance. Their plots are the "before" column of the
[paper comparison](../results/paper-comparison.md).

!!! warning "These are not the current results"
    The plots on these pages predate every correction listed under [Changes](../changes/index.md)
    — among others, the all-hadronic Z→νν band here comes from an unrelated signal sample, the
    luminosity is a fitted 3400 pb⁻¹, and several baseline and signal-region cuts are missing.
    For the corrected output see [Results](../results/index.md).
